# Parking around rue de Terrebonne: supply, measured demand, and the survey's answers in context (note, deck, data)

**Issued by:** the author of these repositories (see the root README). Companion to [`2026-winter-counts-user-frequency/`](../2026-winter-counts-user-frequency/), which did the same for the survey's winter cyclists. Sources: the borough's published study (EXP, *Étude de mobilité sur la rue de Terrebonne*, September 2023, [`2023-exp-mobility-study`](../2023-exp-mobility-study/)): on-street supply (report p. 40), occupancy and vacant places by sector, side and period on Thursday, September 29 and Saturday, October 1, 2022 (Tables 3-2 p. 43 and 3-3 p. 48; PDF page = report page + 11), Table 7-1 p. 88; the cars at Terrebonne's curbs on the 2015 City LiDAR night and on the Québec daytime orthophoto, and the garage-or-driveway reading from the 1954–55 Underwriters' plans and present-day imagery ([`1954-underwriters-plans-terrebonne-garages`](../1954-underwriters-plans-terrebonne-garages/)); the City assessment roll (open data, CC-BY 4.0) for dwellings on Terrebonne and within the Somerled–Monkland band; five winters of snow-removal nights (City snow-removal transactions and ECCC, computed in the author's decision model); the anonymized file of the survey of the Association des piétons et cyclistes de NDG (APC-NDG) as of November 7, 2026 (206 responses; the public filing of November 2, [`2026-11-02-apc-survey-filing`](../2026-11-02-apc-survey-filing/), reports 205), with the hardship criteria of the filing's methods page.

**Date:** analysis of November 7, 2026.

**Question.** The survey's parking answers are counts of respondents. What do the measurements that exist of the same street say about the population they come from, which answers can be checked against a ground truth, and what remains unmeasured?

**Main findings** (intervals and caveats in the note): EXP measured occupancy below its own 80 % threshold at every period in every sector; at 18:30–19:30 on the Thursday, 247 cars occupied 50 % of Terrebonne's places, with 246 vacant there and 298 on the cross-street stubs. The 2015 night count found 183 cars at the curbs, 37 % of the 2022 supply, from 28 % in the west sector to 46 % in the east. Both measurements of demand before the lane are of the order of the one parking band that remains today (about 240 places). On Terrebonne, 72 % of buildings and 70 % of dwellings have a garage or driveway; survey respondents on the street report an off-street place in 18 of 35 households with a vehicle (51 %, interval 35–68 %), those within a block in 43 of 59 (73 %). The survey's 60 street parkers and 35 strict-hardship reports are floors under 573 dwellings on Terrebonne and 5,029 within a block (coverage 6 % and 1 %). Snow removal was pending or under way on about 32 nights a winter in the sector over five winters, 23 of them loading nights. No occupancy has been measured since the lane was built; EXP's mandate of October 6, 2026 (due November 27) analyses supply.
