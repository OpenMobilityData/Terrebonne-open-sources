Lay summary

The survey asked people living on rue de Terrebonne or within a block about their parking. They count the people who replied, not the street. This note sets them beside the measurements on record: the borough's 2023 study (places and their occupancy on two days in 2022), a 2015 night laser survey, the 1954 insurance plans with today's aerial imagery, the City's roll of dwellings, and five winters of snow-loading records.

The 2023 study found half of Terrebonne's places empty on the Thursday evening; the 2015 night count found 183 cars along the street, one place in three. Both, taken before the lane, are close to today's supply on one side. Seven in ten homes on Terrebonne have a garage or driveway; respondents on the street reported one less often, within what 35 answers can show. The survey's 60 street parkers and 35 reports of winter difficulty are floors under 573 dwellings on the street and 5,029 nearby; snow loading constrained parking on about 32 nights a winter, 23 loading nights. Nobody has measured how full the street is now, lane in place.
