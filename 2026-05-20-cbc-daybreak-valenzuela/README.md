## What the interview covers

Her return and parental leave for elected officials; priorities (housing, basic services, security; park chalets); the Namur-Hippodrome site and the Cavendish extension; the borough's mobility plan and its neighbouring municipalities; **the Terrebonne bike path (8:45 to 10:17): « we're really working on finding something that is long term as a solution »; « What we inherited … was a lack of consultation »; « it was really presented as a fait accompli »; « we've taken the time over the last few months to really re-speak to all of the actors involved … But also to the bikers and to the pedestrians »; « I think there is a middle ground … We have concrete scenarios that have been presented to us, and now we're just evaluating which ones we'll be putting in place »**; international outreach at the executive committee.
