# Evidence graph: public statements, documents, data, numerical assertions and assertions on the Terrebonne winter conversion

**Issuer:** the repository's author (a resident of Côte-des-Neiges–Notre-Dame-de-Grâce), from public records. **Date:** started October 20, 2026; updated as the record grows. **Status:** a working map, not a finished document.

**Browse it:** https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-open-sources/2026-evidence-graph/evidence_graph.html (click a node for its full text, its source and its links, each with an arrow to the other end's source; double-click a node or a line to open the source it rests on; "Columns by type" orders each column by date, with the October 6 statements in one column per exchange (the address, then each questioner in webcast order) and rows spaced to each label's height; "Force layout" shows the clusters). Static drawing: [`evidence_graph.svg`](evidence_graph.svg) / [`evidence_graph.png`](evidence_graph.png).

## What it is

A graph kept as data: [`nodes.csv`](nodes.csv) (one row per statement, document, table, numerical assertion, assertion, agent or event, with its date and its source: page of an access-request bundle, recording time stamp, or file) and [`edges.csv`](edges.csv) (one row per relation; a `note` column states the point of difference for every `differs_from`, `unsupported_by` and `does_not_include` edge, shown in the page's side panel and as a tooltip in the SVG; an optional `url` column gives the page or moment that the relation rests on, when it differs from the linked node's own source). [`render_graph.py`](render_graph.py) draws both renderings from the two files (Graphviz; Cytoscape.js).

Every statement node quotes words checked by ear against the recording and gives the time; every document and data node gives the page or file; every numerical-assertion node shows its arithmetic in its label. Relations are a closed list of factual verbs: `attributed_to`, `contained_in`, `released_in`, `withheld_in`, `redacted_in`, `summarized_in`, `precedes`, `implements`, `computed_from`, `states` (a statement puts forward a numerical assertion, or asserts an assertion), `matches` (a statement agrees with a data source), `refers_to`, `echoes`, `sets_aside`, `made_at` (a statement at the sitting, interview or meeting where it was made), `supports`, `undercuts`, `bears_on` (evidence and an assertion), `same_day`, `does_not_include`, `unsupported_by` (a stated figure for which the released record holds no count), `differs_from` and `same_data_other_denominator` (two figures from the same data, two readings). No relation expresses a judgment or a motive.

| Node type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `agent` | who produced or said something |
| `document` | a dated document, released, withheld or redacted |
| `data` | a table or dataset, or a documented absence of one |
| `numerical assertion` | a number put forward in the debate or derived here from the released data, with the operation that produces it shown in its label; the `states` edges say who put it forward, `computed_from` says what it rests on |
| `assertion` | an unattributed proposition that the evidence bears on, worded so that it could be true or false (« The one-direction layout will become year-round »); never a conclusion about a person. Evidence links to it with `supports`, `undercuts` or `bears_on`; a statement or document that asserts it links with `states`. The graph shows what stands on each side and gives no verdict |
| `statement` | a dated public statement, quoted, with its time stamp |
| `event` | a dated step in the process |

## Coverage and checks

The graph is meant to be exhaustive for the question at hand: every checked quotation, every document and table in the record that bears on the winter conversion, and every numerical assertion derived from them. Coverage as of October 22, 2026: the October 6, 2026 sitting in full from the checked transcripts in [`../2026-10-06-borough-council/`](../2026-10-06-borough-council/) (the address; the questions of Shannon Joutel, Raúl Garate, Katherine Lund, Rick Hoge and Steven Jazz with the councillor's answers; the borough mayor's answers to Louis-Eric Trudeau and after Rick Hoge's question; every statement node sourced to its transcript file with the turn's webcast time, its link opening the webcast at that moment; questioners named as the minutes name them), the CBC Daybreak interview of October 9, the City council exchanges of October 19, the borough mayor's November 26, 2025 answer and the mayor's office's December 12, 2025 reply, the Gazette (Oct 7) and Le Devoir (Oct 19), the 2026-581 bundle (mandate, service request, offer, purchase order, kick-off, scheduling e-mails, draft and final note, decks, camera tables, counter table), the 2025-697, 2026-300 and 2025-12847 releases, the 2024 approvals, the 2023 EXP study, the 2021 working committee, the City's February 10, 2026 release, the Telraam sensors, the City open-data detector, and the author's pending requests and reconsideration letter.

`render_graph.py` checks the structure at every build and writes [`validation_report.txt`](validation_report.txt): no orphan node; every statement has `made_at`, `attributed_to` and a source; every numerical assertion has `computed_from`; every bundle-01 document has a release status; every `differs_from`, `unsupported_by` or `does_not_include` edge has a note; and every assertion has evidence on at least one side and belongs to a question; every question in [`questions.csv`](questions.csv) (the written questions prepared for the December 7 sitting, 3.1–3.12, each with the nodes that are its premises) cites nodes that exist. A build with problems still writes the page, and says so.

Rule for additions: a newly checked quotation or newly filed document gets its node and relations in the same commit as the transcript or file.

Added October 23, 2026: the parish of St Monica (question 3.14, assertion `x_hardship`: « The bike lane has caused financial hardship to St Monica's parish »): the 17 T3010 returns and the 2024 return, the key-line table, the parish's own statements 2017–March 2023, the 519 bulletins and the count of the 117 issues since the lane, the Facebook page and its four posts on the lane (2020, 2024), the RNS article of August 13, 2024, the returns of the twelve other parishes of the area, the peer comparison, the detection thresholds, and four numerical assertions (deficits in 7 of 17 years; the 2024 change; the difference from the peers; the detectable effect). Sources: folder `2024-st-monica-cra-t3010/`. Added the same day: the streets around the thirteen churches (the City's bike network and its 2019–2020 and 2024 surveys, the City's parking-sign inventory, the table `peer_streets.csv`, and two numerical assertions: no peer had a lane built on a frontage in 2019–2024, five have no off-street lot; nine poles of St Monica's frontage received a no-stopping panel in July 2024, at most two in any month on a peer frontage), and a third: the share of the curb within 150 m closed to a visitor on a Sunday morning (Sainte-Monique 37 %, Saint-Antonin 48 %, the Mile End reference church 88 %). ## Reproduce

`python3 render_graph.py` in this folder (needs Graphviz `dot`). The page loads Cytoscape.js 3.30.2 from cdnjs and has its data embedded; nothing else is fetched.
