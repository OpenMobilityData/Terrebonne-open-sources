On October 9, 2026, CBC Daybreak interviewed councillor Alexandre Teodoresco about converting one direction of the rue de Terrebonne bike lane to parking in winter. A fact check compares his figures with the public record. From December to March the borough's own counter recorded 40 bicycle passages a day: under one an hour in each direction averaged over 24 hours, about 3 an hour on weekday daytimes. The open-data site he named as the source has no Eco-Counter on Terrebonne. The "50 to 60" parking spaces to be restored appear nowhere in the borough's 508-page file, which contains no parking count. He said the change is being looked at "in a way that will make it permanent"; on October 6 he had called it "a test".
