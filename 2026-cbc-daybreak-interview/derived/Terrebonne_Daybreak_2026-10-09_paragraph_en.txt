On October 9, 2026, CBC Daybreak interviewed councillor Alexandre Teodoresco about converting one direction of the rue de Terrebonne bike lane to parking in winter. A fact check compares his figures with the public record. "Less than one an hour" is the smallest reading the borough's own counter allows: on winter weekdays it averaged 8 an hour at 8 a.m. and never fell below 2 by day, and 4,891 trips were counted from December to March. The open-data site he named as "the source of truth" has no counter on Terrebonne; the borough's counter at Kensington became known only through an access request. The "50 to 60" parking spaces appear nowhere in the borough's 508-page file, which contains no parking count, and sit oddly beside the whole stretch he confirmed. He said the change is being looked at "in a way that will make it permanent"; on October 6 he had called it "a test".
