## What the interview covers

The borough's plan to convert one direction of the protected bike lane on rue de Terrebonne to parking in winter: which side would be kept, the number of parking spaces, winter bicycle counts and their source, the steps before the change (borough council, City council), the timing ("before Christmas"), whether the change could become permanent, and the reopening date. CBC's description of the clip reads: "A bike path in Terrebonne is being shut down this winter to make room for parking. Residents have a lot of thoughts and feelings on the matter. Daybreak took a look at this new bike path controversy with Côte-des-Neiges city councillor, Alexandre Teodoresco. He's the one who presented the compromise to residents."
