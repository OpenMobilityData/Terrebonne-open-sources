## Why this folder exists

The question was whether a cyclist under 15 has been killed in Montréal. The public records do not answer it: the City's collision data (police reports, 2012–2021) and the SAAQ's accident reports carry no age for the people involved, and Statistics Canada's deaths by cause are published for Canada, not for a city. A search of the press returned the five articles below. They concern four events; two more events were added on November 18, 2026 (see above), so the folder now holds eight articles on six events. The folder records what each article says; it is not a census of such events, and an event that the press did not report, or that this one search did not return, is not here.

## What the captures do not establish

- The outcome of the Côte-Saint-Paul collision of May 1, 2016 after May 9, 2016.
- The outcome of the charge laid in April 2025 after the Ahuntsic-Cartierville collision, and the findings of the police investigation at Saint-Hyacinthe.
- How deaths of riders of electric scooters are counted in official statistics. They are not counted as cyclists: in the international classification of causes of death (ICD-10), the user of a scooter is a pedestrian, and a « motorized bicycle » is a motorcycle, not a pedal cycle.
- Whether other cyclists under 15 were killed in Montréal in these or other years. One search of the press is the only basis of this folder; the Bureau du coroner holds the record of every such death with the age and the place.
- The names of the young people are in the articles; they are not repeated here.
