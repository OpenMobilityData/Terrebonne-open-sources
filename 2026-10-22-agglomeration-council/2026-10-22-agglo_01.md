**David Gamper (written question, read by the chair)** 29:58  
On Terrebonne Street in CDN-NDG, a one-way street, the borough proposes for this coming winter to remove one of the two protected cycle lanes and have cyclists in that direction ride in a single traffic lane about 3.5 m wide between two rows of parked cars. The minimum separation of one metre required to pass a cyclist is not available, and this forces either unsafe overtaking or cars to drive behind cyclists at their speed. Moreover, in snow and ice conditions, uncleared edges often force cyclists to ride further out into the road, making it even more dangerous for cars to overtake. my question is: has the SPVM been consulted on this layout? And what is their opinion? Will they carry out increased surveillance ?

**Councillor Teodoresco** 31:18  
Merci, Monsieur le Président. Thank you for the question, Mr. Gamper. We have to start by acknowledging that many residents felt that their concerns regarding the Terrebonne bike path were not heard by the previous administration. We heard this message clearly, and we want to make, to have a different approach, not a dogmatic one, but a very pragmatic one. I know it's very difficult for some to understand our approach, but nevertheless, you will see it in action. Residents expect us to listen and to act, and this is exactly what we're doing. Our approach is simple, listen, act, evaluate the results, and adjust as needed. That is why we're introducing a new winter configuration on the Terrebonne bike path and testing it over the coming months. We will also consult residents to hear their feedback and better understand how this configuration responds to the needs of the neighborhood. And to make sure that our evaluation is based not only on feedback but also on objective data, I have asked that our new generation of traffic counters be deployed on Terrebonne Street starting this winter. This will help us better measure how the street is being used and the impact of the new configuration. Public safety is paramount in our endeavor to find the right balance.

32:29 And I say this not only as an elected official but also as an avid cyclist and as the father of two young cyclists who use the bike path. Their safety matters deeply to me, just as the safety of everyone who uses the Terrebonne bike path. And we're not looking to impose a one-size-fits-all solution, Mr. President. We want to work with all residents to find the best possible balance between active mobility, road safety, and the residents' quality of life. Thank you very much.
