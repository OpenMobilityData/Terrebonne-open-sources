# Children and teenagers killed or gravely injured on the road in and around Montréal, in the press, 2012–2020

**Issued by:** CTV Montreal and CBC News (the articles).
**Dates:** September 2012 to August 2020.
**Obtained:** the five articles were found by a web search on November 16, 2026 and opened the same day in the repository author's browser, which saved each page as a PDF. Each article is © its publisher. The captures in `raw/` are cropped to the headline, the date and the lead, for the purposes of criticism and review (fair dealing, *Copyright Act*, s. 29.1), with the source credited; the photograph of one of the young people that accompanies an article is left out. The full pages are kept privately and are not reproduced.
**Link status (November 16, 2026):** the addresses below are those returned by the search. The two older CTV addresses redirect to `www.ctvnews.ca/montreal/article/…`; the CBC pages refused a program and opened in a browser. No snapshot was requested from the Internet Archive.

## Why this folder exists

The question was whether a cyclist under 15 has been killed in Montréal. The public records do not answer it: the City's collision data (police reports, 2012–2021) and the SAAQ's accident reports carry no age for the people involved, and Statistics Canada's deaths by cause are published for Canada, not for a city. A search of the press returned the five articles below. They concern four events. The folder records what each article says; it is not a census of such events, and an event that the press did not report, or that this one search did not return, is not here.

## What the captures do not establish

- The outcome of the Côte-Saint-Paul collision of May 1, 2016 after May 9, 2016.
- Whether other cyclists under 15 were killed in Montréal in these or other years. One search of the press is the only basis of this folder; the Bureau du coroner holds the record of every such death with the age and the place.
- The names of the young people are in the articles; they are not repeated here.
