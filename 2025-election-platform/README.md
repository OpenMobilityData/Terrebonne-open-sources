# The 2025 election platform of Ensemble Montréal, and the borough's cycling page

**Issuer:** Ensemble Montréal (the party of the borough mayor and of the Loyola councillor elected on November 2, 2025); the City of Montréal for the borough page. **Date:** platform PDF dated October 16, 2025 (65 pages); borough page updated October 24, 2025. **Source:** `https://ensemblemtl.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Ensemble-MTL-plateforme.pdf`, downloaded October 21, 2026, snapshot requested at web.archive.org/save the same day; `https://montreal.ca/articles/les-amenagements-cyclables-cote-des-neiges-notre-dame-de-grace-45741` (and the English page), captured with headless Chrome on October 21, 2026, snapshot requested the same day. **Link status:** both live on October 21, 2026.

## What the platform says about cycling, parking and Terrebonne

The platform's cycling commitment, in the section on safe mobility ([p. 27](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-open-sources/2025-election-platform/raw/Ensemble-MTL-plateforme_2025-10.pdf#page=27)): « Réaliser un état des lieux du réseau cyclable afin d'évaluer les enjeux de sécurité des usagers (cyclistes, personnes piétonnes, automobilistes). » Its general principle ([p. 25](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-open-sources/2025-election-platform/raw/Ensemble-MTL-plateforme_2025-10.pdf#page=25)): « chaque aménagement, qu'il s'agisse d'une rue, d'un trottoir, d'une ligne de transport collectif ou d'une piste cyclable, tient compte des besoins de tous les usagers ainsi que des réalités économiques locales. »

The borough section for Côte-des-Neiges—Notre-Dame-de-Grâce ([p. 55](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-open-sources/2025-election-platform/raw/Ensemble-MTL-plateforme_2025-10.pdf#page=55)) lists: a traffic plan and mitigation for the REM's Canora station; securing dangerous intersections near schools and the de la Savane, Namur, Plamondon and Snowdon metro stations; redoing avenue Linton and the Bates roundabout; lighting, crime prevention, housing inspections, flood prevention, the Empress Theatre, a « Route du Souvenir », support for small business.

The words « Terrebonne », « retirer » or « démanteler » applied to a cycling facility do not appear in the document. (Checked by text search of the whole PDF on October 21, 2026; the only parking commitments are free permits for certain workers and more free spaces during snow loading, in the Rosemont section.)

## What the borough's cycling page says

The City's page « Les aménagements cyclables à Côte-des-Neiges–Notre-Dame-de-Grâce » (updated October 24, 2025) lists among the facilities: « de Terrebonne de St-Ignatius à Girouard (voie cyclable unidirectionnelle protégée par le stationnement ou par des bollards) ».

## Why it is here

The councillor said at the borough council on October 6, 2026, « we have made a commitment and we have to act on this promise », and at City council on October 19, « c'est ce qu'on s'est engagé à faire en campagne et tout de suite après qu'on est arrivé à la mairie, de revoir la configuration ». The platform is the written campaign commitment; what it says is a review of the network's safety. Statements by candidates during the campaign (social media, flyers, local press) are not collected here; anyone holding one with its date is invited to add it.
