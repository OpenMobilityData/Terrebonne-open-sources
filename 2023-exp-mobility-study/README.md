## What it is

The mobility study commissioned by the borough before the 2024 reconfiguration of rue de Terrebonne (one-way street with protected bike lanes in both directions, St-Ignatius to Girouard). According to the borough's press release of October 30, 2023: the firm was mandated by the borough council in September 2022; the study cost $150,000 before taxes, with a $50,000 contribution from Infrastructure Canada's Active Transportation Fund; EXP carried out new counts of vehicles, bicycles and pedestrians at 17 intersections and characterized more than 1,100 parking spaces, the counts and parking surveys being made in October 2022; the study area runs from Somerled to Monkland and from St-Ignatius to Girouard.

## Why it is here

The 2026 record released under access request 2026-581 (see the companion repository) refers to this study's traffic projections and to the "two mandates" question: the 2023 study was a feasibility and design study for a project the borough had decided to pursue, and the 2026 note was commissioned to recommend a change to that project. Readers of either analysis should be able to open the 2023 study itself.
