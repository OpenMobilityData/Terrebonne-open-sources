## What the documents say

**June 22, 2020.** The borough director recommends approving the plan of "rues, secteurs et corridors à vocation familiale et active", a COVID-19 measure for the summer of 2020, with a budget of $130,000 from the borough's surplus. The tables of interventions include Terrebonne, Belmore to Girouard, 2,400 m. These are the temporary bicycle lanes on both sides of the street referred to in the September motion.

**September 8, 2020.** The motion's recitals state, among other things, that no vote had borne specifically on lanes on both sides of Terrebonne, "lesquelles ont supprimé toutes les places de stationnement sur cette rue"; that petitions demanding their immediate removal had "été signées par environ 2 000 résidents du quartier"; that the councillor for Notre-Dame-de-Grâce had publicly said that removing all parking was not an optimal solution and recommended a traffic study of a one-way street with parking on one side and a bidirectional lane on the other; and that "au cours des vingt ans d'histoire de l'arrondissement, peu de gestes de son administration ont provoqué un tel tollé". It moves that the borough's services remove the lanes from September 9, 2020; that the council, "sans toutefois cautionner précisément" a one-way conversion, invite the City to study its feasibility; that the borough count cyclists on the existing lanes of the NDG part of the borough over the following year; and that the council apologize to the citizens inconvenienced.

**What followed.** The council amended the motion and adopted, as resolution CA20 170210, the creation of the Comité de travail pour la mobilité sur la rue de Terrebonne (see [`2020-2021-working-committee/`](../2020-2021-working-committee/)), whose note of June 2021 quotes the one-way-study paragraph of the amended motion.
