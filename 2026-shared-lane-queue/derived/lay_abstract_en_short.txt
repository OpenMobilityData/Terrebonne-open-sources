Lay summary

In the borough's winter layout, cyclists going the same way as the cars would ride in a traffic lane too narrow for a car to pass them with the one metre the law requires, so drivers who catch up stay behind. Using the borough's counter (about 120 cars an hour at the winter morning rush) and assumed speeds of 15 km/h for the cyclist and 25 km/h for cars, a cyclist typically has two or three cars behind by Girouard: about one on a quiet morning, about eight on a busy one with a slow cyclist on snow. About 12 of the 120 peak-hour cars would follow a cyclist, each losing at most about a minute and a half per kilometre.
