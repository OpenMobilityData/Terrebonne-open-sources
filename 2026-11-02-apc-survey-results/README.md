## What the document says

The letter presents « les premiers résultats » of an online survey open from October 15 to the morning of November 2, 2026 (205 responses, 161 kept after the rules stated in the methods page), on parking and cycling on rue de Terrebonne in winter, and gives, with its tables: where respondents live, their household vehicles and off-street parking, last winter's parking experience, winter cycling on Terrebonne and perceived safety in a shared lane, and the preference among the three options the borough was considering. On the preference question: 91 of the 161 kept chose to keep the protected lane in both directions, 57 to convert one direction to parking for the winter and 12 to allow parking only during snow-loading operations; the 44 excluded split 17, 19 and 7. The letter states that « les chiffres de préférence décrivent qui a répondu, et non le quartier ». An annex gives the reach of the survey links by channel class.

## Note on the version

The printed copies handed over on November 2 differed from these files by one sentence in the paragraph « Préférence entre les trois options », on the number of comments asking for no bike path at all; the association sent the present version to the clerk on November 3, 2026, with a note stating the correction. All other figures are identical.
