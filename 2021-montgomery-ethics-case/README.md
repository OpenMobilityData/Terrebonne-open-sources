# The Montgomery ethics case, 2020–2023: the public record

Public documents, not obtained under the Act, on the proceedings between the City of Montréal, the Commission municipale du Québec (CMQ) and Sue Montgomery, borough mayor of Côte-des-Neiges–Notre-Dame-de-Grâce from November 2017 to November 2021. The folder exists because the borough council that approved the Terrebonne working committee (2020–21) and the 2024 project sat through these events, and because the case is the reference on ethics standards for the borough's elected officials. Each item is described and quoted; nothing is evaluated and no motive is imputed.
