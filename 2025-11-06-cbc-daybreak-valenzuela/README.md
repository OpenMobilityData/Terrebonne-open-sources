## What the interview covers

The election result and the campaign; priorities (basic services, housing, security; the 2026 budget); the Namur-Hippodrome site and the borough's housing stock (Project Chester on Walkley); the Cavendish extension and the BAPE; homelessness and Martin-Luther-King park; **the Terrebonne bike path and the audit: « So the current position for us is that we wait for the results. We keep what is there as a network » (7:55), the audit « already prepared by the administration », « within the first 100 days » (10:12)**; the council's composition and the recount in Côte-des-Neiges.
