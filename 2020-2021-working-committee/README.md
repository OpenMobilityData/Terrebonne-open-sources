## What the documents say

**The mandate.** Resolution CA20 170210 creates "un groupe de travail … ayant pour mandat de proposer des recommandations qui visent l'amélioration de la mobilité sur la rue de Terrebonne", chaired by the councillor for Notre-Dame-de-Grâce, Peter McQueen, with borough staff, school boards, the religious institutions of the street, the HLM Terrebonne and residents of stated profiles; it gives the committee access to the usage statistics of the temporary lane and to the borough's survey data, and asks for a final report by February 2021. The committee's note quotes the paragraph of the amended motion under which it reports: "QUE, sans privilégier précisément la possibilité de rendre la rue de Terrebonne à sens unique, le conseil d'arrondissement invite la Ville-centre à effectuer une étude de la circulation pour évaluer la faisabilité de la transformation de cette rue en sens unique …".

**The recommendations (note of June 13, 2021).** The committee met eight times between November 19, 2020 and May 19, 2021. Its note is in three parts:

- *A. Recommandations unanimes.* That the council invite the City to study "le scénario 5bis": rue de Terrebonne one-way eastbound between Girouard and, as alternatives to the west, Grand, Madison or Benny, taking into account the effect on neighbouring streets. The note records that three members, "à l'instar de l'Ombudsman de Montréal", had serious reservations about any transformation west of Grand because of the institutions there, and that three other members and the chair would have wanted the lane to reach Benny. "En conjonction et en complément", the committee also unanimously recommends that the City study bike lanes on avenue Somerled between Grand and Walkley (scenarios A2 or A2bis).
- *B. Recommandations majoritaires.* By simple majority, that the City also study "le scénario 1" over the same section, again in combination with the Somerled scenarios. On resuming its work, five members wished to wait for the City's traffic study (four of them also for an independent count), three to continue without waiting.
- *C. Mentions faisant l'objet d'un vote partagé.* Nine propositions on which members divided, with the tallies given in the note: 4–4 (the chair later changing his vote to "pour"), 5–3, 7–1, 4–3 with one amendment, 4–3, 4–4, 5–3, 5–3, 4–4. The note states that the form's percentages should have accounted for the absence of the English Montreal School Board's representative. Proposition 3 (7–1) "a mené aux recommandations unanimes ci-dessus".

**The vote image** shows the twelve propositions as put to the form, including A (that nothing be changed on Terrebonne in 2021 and that an independent professional count be commissioned: 50%–50%), C (the one-way feasibility study: 87.5% in favour), 2 (a painted lane and a protected lane on Somerled between Grand and Walkley: 50% for, 37.5% against, 12.5% proposing a change) and 5 (a one-way pilot on Terrebonne in summer 2021: 50%–50%).

**Tabling.** The recommendations were tabled at the council sitting of June 21, 2021 as item 61.03, "Prendre acte du dépôt", signed by the borough director on June 15, 2021.
