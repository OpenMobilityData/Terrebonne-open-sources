## The agenda as posted: no item on rue de Terrebonne

The agenda list (11 pages) names no item on rue de Terrebonne, on a bike lane or on a « vélorue ». Its traffic and parking items are 12.01 (support for a truck-traffic by-law of the borough of Ville-Marie), 40.05 (adoption of a by-law amending the traffic and parking by-law, C-4.1, for daily permits and commercial establishments), 40.07 to 40.10 (four parking-permit ordinances) and 40.11 (an ordinance on a left turn). Items 30.02 and 30.03 accept, under article 85.1 of the Charter, offers of service of two other boroughs (Rosemont–La Petite-Patrie: maintenance of lighting, signage and road marking for 2027; Ville-Marie: a public-works team).

The file of November 2, 2026 for the offer to City council on rue de Terrebonne ([`2026-11-02-borough-council/`](../2026-11-02-borough-council/), file 1266880005, calendar) reads: « Décembre 2026 : Résolution du conseil d'arrondissement pour les changements de signalisation ». No such resolution is on the agenda as posted on November 27. An agenda is adopted at the opening of the sitting (item 10.02) and items can be added or withdrawn then.
