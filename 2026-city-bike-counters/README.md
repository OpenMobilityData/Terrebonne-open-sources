# The City's bicycle counters and intersection detectors: supplier, procurement and the 2026 change of technology

Public documents, not obtained under the Act, on how the City of Montréal counts bicycles at its own sites (the network reported on the open-data portal), as distinct from the borough's loop counter at Terrebonne/Kensington. Each item is described and quoted; nothing is evaluated.

## Why this folder exists

The borough's winter figures for Terrebonne come from a borough-owned Eco-Counter loop (requests 2025-697, 2026-300, 2026-581). The City's own network is a separate system: inductive loops managed under contract by Eco-Compteur until March 2026, and, from 2025–26, camera detectors mounted on traffic-light poles bought through the City's intersection-detection tenders. The councillor for Loyola, as the executive committee member for optimization, municipal performance and innovation, is quoted on that transition (Eco-Compteur release of June 4, 2026; Radio-Canada, May 29, 2026). This folder holds the primary records of the transition.

## The sequence, from the documents

- 2017–2022: framework agreements for traffic-light and intelligent-transport equipment (Électroméga, Orange Trafic, Tacel, MI-8); 2018 and 2020: vehicle detectors at signalized intersections (Tacel; Tacel and MI-8); 2021: « systèmes de détection MI-BLOC vidéo thermique » for the Pie-IX bus corridor (MI-8); November 2022: Tacel framework for « détecteurs véhiculaires, de comptage, classification » ($4.5 M).
- June 4, 2025: direct purchase of nine CITIX-AI Evo cameras and five infrared counters from Éco-Compteur ($125,443).
- April–June 2025: tender 25-21000 for traffic-light surveillance cameras, later cancelled; May–August 2025: tender 25-21046 for an intersection safety-and-mobility analysis system, awarded to Tacel.
- November 10, 2025: tender 25-21195 for intersection detectors in three lots, lot 1 thermal-video presence detectors, lot 2 pedestrian and bicycle detectors.
- Mid-March 2026: daily updates of the counter data stop; the Eco-Compteur service contract is not renewed (Radio-Canada).
- March 23, 2026: CM26 0337 awards lot 1 to Tacel and lot 2 to Éco-Compteur, unanimously.
- May 29, 2026: the City puts the counts back online on its own platform (Radio-Canada; La Presse the same day, not reproduced here).
- June 4, 2026: Eco-Compteur announces the renewed partnership and the CITIX-AI Evo system.

Not in this folder: the tender documents of 25-21195 (the specifications DTI-6D-4718 and DTI-6D-4724), which SEAO serves to registered users; the La Presse articles of April 1 and May 29, 2026 (paywalled).
