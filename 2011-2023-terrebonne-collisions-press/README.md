# Collisions on and around rue de Terrebonne in the press, 2011–2023

**Issued by:** CBC News, CTV Montreal, Global News, the Montreal Gazette and The Suburban (the articles); the captures were made by a resident and kept with the petition material of 2011.
**Dates:** May 2011 to June 2023.
**Obtained:** screen captures assembled by the repository's author from a resident's collection, received October 26, 2026; the capture files carry no creation dates of their own. Each article is © its publisher; the captures are reproduced here, cropped to the headline, date and lead, for the purposes of criticism and review (fair dealing, *Copyright Act*, s. 29.1), with the source credited. Links are given where the article was found online on October 26, 2026; the others are identified by outlet, author, date and headline as shown in the capture.

## Why this folder exists

A recurring argument against the Terrebonne lanes is that no serious collision ever happened there. The press record kept by residents shows two deaths on the street before the 2024 lanes (a three-year-old at Terrebonne and Grand on May 31, 2011; an 84-year-old pedestrian at Terrebonne and Girouard on September 3, 2019), a corner at Terrebonne and Kensington with « about a dozen accidents … over the past five years » in 2011, and a residents' petition against dangerous car traffic on Terrebonne in June 2011. The folder describes the captures; the analysis of collision patterns before a cyclist death, from the SPVM open data, is planned separately. This README describes and quotes; it does not evaluate.
