Lay summary

The borough's winter figures for rue de Terrebonne count passages at one point, Kensington: one bicycle crossing in one direction. They do not count people. This note uses four sensors along the street to ask how many people cycle it in winter and how often, and compares the answer with the APC-NDG survey of October and November 2026.

From December 1, 2025 to February 28, 2026 Kensington recorded 3,232 passages, about 42 on a weekday and 20 on a weekend day; some 11 a day are matched morning and evening trips, the mark of commuting. The street is busier at its east end: on dry days Draper counts 1.3 times Kensington and Girouard 1.6 times. No counter gives a head count without knowing how often each person rides, but at least 154 different people cycled past King Edward on March 9, 2026 and at least 42 past Kensington on December 3, 2025. The survey's 87 winter cyclists account for about 60 per cent of the passages counted: consistent with the count, and a floor under the number of people, not a measure of it.
