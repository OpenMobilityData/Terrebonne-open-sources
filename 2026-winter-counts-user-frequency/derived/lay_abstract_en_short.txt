Lay summary

The borough's winter figures for rue de Terrebonne count passages, not people, at one point, Kensington: one bicycle crossing, one direction. This note asks, from four sensors, how many people cycle it in winter and how often, and compares the answer with the 2026 survey of the Association des piétons et cyclistes de NDG (APC-NDG).

From December 2025 to February 2026 Kensington recorded 3,232 passages, 42 on a weekday, 20 on a weekend day; 11 a day are matched morning and evening trips, the mark of commuting. The street is busier at its east end: on dry days Draper counts 1.3 times Kensington and Girouard 1.6 times. Without knowing how often each person rides, no counter gives a head count, but at least 154 different people passed King Edward on March 9, 2026 and at least 42 passed Kensington on December 3, 2025. The survey's 87 winter cyclists account for about 60 per cent of the passages counted: consistent with the count, a floor under the number of people, not a measure of it.
