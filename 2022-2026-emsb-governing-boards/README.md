# The Terrebonne lane in the records of the English Montreal School Board: governing-board and council minutes 2022–2026, and the EMSB letter of November 22, 2024

**Issued by:** English Montreal School Board (EMSB): the Governing Board of Willingdon Elementary School (junior and senior campuses, Draper and Royal at Terrebonne), the Governing Board of St. Monica Elementary School (6440 rue de Terrebonne), and the EMSB Council of Commissioners (regular and special board meetings). The letter of November 22, 2024 was published on the « EMSB Chair's Blog - Joe Ortona » (emsb-chair.blogspot.com). **Dates:** meetings of the school years 2022–23 to 2026–27 (to October 29, 2026). **Obtained:** October 29, 2026, from the schools' and the board's governance pages (no login; the PDFs are served from cdn.aglty.io), every file with HTTP 200; the index pages as fetched are in each body's `index-pages/` folder (HTML, with a text copy). The letter was printed to PDF with headless Chrome from the blog and from its Wayback Machine capture of the same day. **Link status:** every source URL answered 200 on October 29, 2026; Wayback captures per file in [`derived/documents.csv`](derived/documents.csv) (`wayback_url`, `wayback_latest_existing`).

**Scope.** Every governing-board document posted for the two schools and every board-meeting minute of the Council of Commissioners for those years was downloaded, searched and hashed (SHA-256). The 35 documents that mention Terrebonne (other than the school's own letterhead address, « 6440 rue Terrebonne » / « 5870 de Terrebonne ») or a bike lane, bike path or piste cyclable are filed here; the other 130 are listed in `documents.csv` with their URL, hash and page count, not reproduced. Executive-committee minutes of the board were not collected. Names are given as the minutes print them: the minutes are public records published by the schools and the board.

**Text.** Some of the filed school documents are born-digital (St. Monica from January 2025); most are scans, read with tesseract (300 dpi) or from the scanner's own text layer. Every passage quoted below from a school document was checked against the page image on October 29, 2026 (`text_source` and `check_note` in [`derived/passages.csv`](derived/passages.csv)); the minutes' own spellings are kept as printed (« Terrbonne », « Canvendish », « bsses », « city counsellor »).
