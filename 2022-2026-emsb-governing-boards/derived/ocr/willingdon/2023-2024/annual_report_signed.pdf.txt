Chairperson's message:


The two main strengths of Willingdon's Governing Board are the level of involvement of our
parents, faculty and community representatives, and the positive working relationship between
the members of the GB and the school administration. Although this was only my first year as
chairperson, it was clear to me from our first meeting that all of us share a commitment to
ensuring that Willingdon remains an inclusive and safe environment that meets the needs of all
of our students. Willingdon's teachers and staff offer a variety of worthwhile educational
opportunities and extra-curricular actlvities. One ofthe main roles ofthe Governing Board isto
support all of the great work that they do for our children. Moreover, we are fortunate to have

=== page 4 (text layer) ===
an active Home and School that raises money to ensure that all of our students, regardless of
their socio-economic status, can fully participate in everything that Willingdon has to offer. In
addition, H&S organizes amazing events, such as the Student Film Fesft'val and the Year-End
School Bash, which allow our students to showcase their talent and contribute to a sense of
community and belonging within the school. Our community representatives regularly attend
meetings and share important information about ongoing challenges facing the wider
community. Their involvement has been essential to ensuring that Willingdon remains
connected to the NDG community. Our school board commissioner has attended every
meeting, which has been valuable to the members for us to get updates on actions taken by the
EMSB regarding ongoing legal matters and allocation of resources. Moreover, the concerns that
we express and the issues we raise get shared with the other school board commissioners.
Finally, I am very appreciative of the extent to which our parent members and faculty
representatives have contributed to the smooth functioning ofthe Governing Board. From
volunteering to take the minutes to reading the Land Acknowledgment to chairing a meeting in
my absence, not to mention responding to several last-minute requests to cast e-votes,
members have demonstrated their generosjty and willingness to serve the Willingdon
community. Moving forward, 1 hope that with a new principal coming in next year, we are able
to maintain the same level of cooperation and collegiality, while at the same time not shying
away from raising concerns we have about the safety and wellbeing of our children.
