-Alex: She and her kids have had near-misses with cyclists. Some sections are more

Halloween: Thanks to volunteers for this massive undertaking. Great feedback from

their own activities.

Community Book Fair was successful with $2,000 of sales (vs. approx. $750 of
rewards). We will continue to find ways to up the title selection for Senior Campus.

within networks.

fundraising activities green, but parents from past graduating classes expressed
interest in grad items for themselves. Alternatively, we could organize a “Willingdon
2025”-branded gear to the entire school community, capitalizing on a variation of
the ’25 grad logo. To be discussed with the Home & School team.

Grad Committee will host a family movie night in the Senior campus gym, including
a concession stand, targeting Gr. 4 to 6 families. Allows Grade 4 parents a sneak
peak of Senior. Dates: TBD — targeting May 2025. Pay what you can at the door, $2-
