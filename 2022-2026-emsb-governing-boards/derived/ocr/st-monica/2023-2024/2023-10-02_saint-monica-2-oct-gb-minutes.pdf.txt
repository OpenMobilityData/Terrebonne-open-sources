7.1.5 Science Curriculum
Curriculum is to be delivered in French however Principal reports current problem with
getting a French teacher to deliver the subject content. Present teacher is teaching
science in English and doing very well with the subject content and providing engaging
science learning experiences ensuring that the Ministry guidelines are followed.
Principal has included science in her plan when she hires tutors for the year to ensure
that students receive additional exposure to French language usage.
