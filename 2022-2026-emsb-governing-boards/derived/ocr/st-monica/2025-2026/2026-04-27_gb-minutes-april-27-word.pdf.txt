   •   Students are in preparation for the spring concert (June 5th)
   •   Prep is well underway for Grade 6 graduation (June 22nd)
   •   Our Grade 3 to 6 students have resumed swimming at the NDG Sports
       Complex. The Canadian Swim to Survive program, designed for Grade 3
       students, is cancelled due to a lack of staff at the site.
   • Students have begun practicing for the annual Halo Race taking place on May
       13 on Mount Royal; 56 students are currently registered.
   • Annual field trip for Grade 5 students to CEPSUM (Université de Montréal –
       2100 Édouard Montpetit) on May 14.
   • SCIENCE: Cycle II students (Grades 3 & 4) have been actively exploring the
       states of matter in science, learning to identify solids, liquids, and gases while
       observing and discussing how matter can change from one state to another
       through simple, hands-on examples.
   • LANGUAGES: Students diving into food-themed learning by practicing recipe
       writing, using cooking action verbs, identifying ingredients, and following clear
       procedural steps to understand how-to texts. The classes are creating a
       bilingual cookbook that includes their family recipes; all recipes will be written in
       French and the reason why the recipe was chosen will be explained in English.
   • LIBRARY: Bi-weekly visits to the Benny Library to explore books, develop
       reading skills, and participate in an engaging History Box activity in social
       studies.
