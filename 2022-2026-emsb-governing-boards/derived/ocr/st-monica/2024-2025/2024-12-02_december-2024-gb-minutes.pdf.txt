6. Reports
6.1. Principal’s Report

e Welcome to December! 19 days away from winter break.

e Diana Bavis, Staff assistant, is on personal leave and is now replaced by Jim
Daskalakis... to help Mr. Mangal with administrative duties. He's a retired math
teacher and principal.

e Sandra Flores resigned and her last day was on Friday November 29. She was a
fabulous daycare technician who established strong bonds with everyone in the
St. Monica's community.

e First session of in-person parent-teacher interviews went great. Second set of
interviews will be virtual.

o Incident where a parent confronted another parent about a situation that was
already resolved.

o There is a need for parents to model conflict resolution and respect the
structure of the school.

o We need to change behaviours in our community
e Great book fair! 3 cases of students who stole merchandise and these students
were spoken to.
e Great movie night that had an impressive turn-out. Huge thanks to Margaret and
Peter Ford for setting up the big screen and projector, and for allowing us to use
their popcorn maker and cotton candy maker.
e Educational Project Update
o Teachers need more support when students go home. Many times work is
sent home and not completed. Teachers want to set up an evening to teach
parents how to handle and manage homework. To improve student success
rates, we all need to start working together — teacher, administrators and
parents.

o Need to make more effort with French — we're launching French Fridays at
the school where everyone is speaking French on Fridays. Focusing on
writing, reading and comprehension.
