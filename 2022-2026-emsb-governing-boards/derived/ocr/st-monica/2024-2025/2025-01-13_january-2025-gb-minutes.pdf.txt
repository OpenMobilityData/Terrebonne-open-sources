6. Reports
6.1. Principal’s Report
  • December & January Events
    o The breakfast with Santa event in December was a huge success because of
      the efforts of the teams at Breakfast Club and the Generations Foundation; Val
      for decorations and the whole team.
    o A new TV is installed in the foyer. It’s used to announce events and to recognize
      accomplishments.
    o Last Friday, Grade 6 students went to Rosemount High School for a kick-off for
      the month of Music (January) to perform Ode to Joy. Ms. Allison and the
      students did a phenomenal job representing our school and community.
    o Later in January, we’ll be hosting an open house and a literacy evening. It is a
      strategic priority for our education plan to invite existing parents to share tools
      and kickstart literacy at home.
    o In February, We’ll host a series of Wednesday workshops for our Cycle 2
      students, organized by This360. The workshops will focus on social-emotional
      tools.
  • Update to code of conduct
    o Revamping the look of it to make things easier to find.
    o Will be presented at the next governing board meeting for approval, after it’s
      been presented to the teachers and staff council.
  • Trips (Day and overnight)
    o The traditional grade 6 overnight trip is cancelled. It requires a classroom
      teacher to attend for risk and liability reasons, but there are currently no
      teachers available to go. Grade 6 students to be offered an extended day trip
      that includes a team-building escape room, a disco boat and a restaurant
      dinner. Students should return to the school before 6:30 p.m.
  • Subject time allocation
    o Starting to look forward to next year’s planning and allocating time accordingly to
      each subject. Some subjects are more flexible than others.
    o Currently, the CCQ is being taught in French, but the government requires the
      content of this course in a student’s mother tongue.
    o Science and Technology should be taught in French. Currently, we do not have a
      teacher to teach science in French.
