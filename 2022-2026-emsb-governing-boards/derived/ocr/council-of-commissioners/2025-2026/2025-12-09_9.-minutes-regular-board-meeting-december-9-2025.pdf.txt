1.   Land Acknowledgement

     We/I would like to acknowledge that the English Montreal School Board is located on the traditional,
     ancestral, and unceded territory of the Kanienʼkehá꞉ka*. We are grateful to live, learn, and work in
     Tiohtiá:ke*, which has long been a site of meeting and exchange for many First Nations, and is currently
     home to diverse groups of Indigenous and other peoples.

     As a School Board, it is important that we acknowledge the historic and ongoing role of educational
     institutions in perpetuating injustices against Indigenous communities. We are committed to:

        •   Educating staff and students about the true history and current realities of the Indigenous Peoples of
            what we now call Canada.
        •   Celebrating the cultures, contributions, and knowledge of the many First Nations, Inuit, and Métis
            communities.
        •   Listening to and amplifying Indigenous voices.
        •   And partnering with local Indigenous community members.

     We offer this acknowledgement as a first step and public commitment to our ongoing process towards
     reconciliation and justice for Indigenous peoples and communities.

A statement was made by the Chair, Mr. Joe Ortona, regarding public question period moving forward.
The rules have always been clear: the question period is open to members of the public who wish to attend
in person and ask their questions. We have always respected that rule. People were here tonight, and they
had the opportunity to ask their questions, which poses no issue for me whatsoever.
During COVID, meetings were broadcast but not open to in‑person attendance. Because of that exceptional
circumstance, we temporarily allowed written submissions. The rule itself did not change, but we made an
accommodation so people could still participate. For those who submitted questions in writing, I read them
aloud and provided answers. That process, however, is not part of the official rules.
This will be the last month that I accept written questions. For several months now, I’ve received
submissions that I am not comfortable reading. If I choose not to read them, I’m accused of censorship or
even threatened with being taken to the Ethics Commissioner. If I do read them, I worry that the wording,
spoken in my voice, could be misrepresented.
Going forward, anyone who wishes to ask a question may do so by attending in person. This is a public
forum. As long as questions are asked respectfully, follow the rules of decorum, and meet basic standards
of decency, anyone is welcome to participate. But I will no longer accept questions submitted in writing.

17.
      Governance Structure of the Comité de gestion de la taxe scolaire de l’île de Montréal (CGTSIM)

      WHEREAS the Comité de gestion de la taxe scolaire de l’île de Montréal (“CGTSIM”) serves an
      important function in collecting and allocating school tax revenue among school organizations of the island
      of Montréal, as well as managing the cash and loans of those organizations, for the benefit of all students
      on the island of Montréal;

      WHEREAS the decisions of the CGTSIM on the allocation of funding affect the services that can be
      provided by the EMSB to its students;

      WHEREAS commissioners of the EMSB, elected by the English-speaking community in its territory, have
      represented the EMSB on the CGTSIM since the creation of the EMSB in 1998;

      WHEREAS Bill 94, adopted on October 30, 2025, replaces the commissioner designated by the EMSB
      on the board of the CGTSIM with the director general of the school board or an assistant director general
      designated by them;

      WHEREAS section 23 of the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms (“Charter”) protects the minority
      language community’s right to management and control of minority language education and facilities,
      including an exclusive right to make decisions regarding the allocation of funds for minority language
      education;
      WHEREAS in the Bill 40 case, the Québec Court of Appeal declared that provisions replacing the chair
      of the council of commissioners with the director general as the spokesperson of the board were found to
      violate s. 23 of the Charter and to be inapplicable to English-language school boards (at paragraphs 183-
      186 of the judgment);

      WHEREAS the governance structure of the CGTSIM infringes the minority language community’s right
      to designate its representatives to exercise management and control over funds for minority language
      education;

=== page 10 (text layer) ===
      IT WAS MOVED BY MS. MARIA CORSI AND RESOLVED THAT the Council of Commissioners of
      the EMSB mandates Power Law to initiate a legal proceeding on behalf of the EMSB to challenge the
      governance structure of the CGTSIM.

      Vote: 14-1-0, Adopted (Ms. J. Houde-Woytiuk voted against)

18.   Affirmation Designating February 2026 as a Celebration of Black History Month

      WHEREAS many Canadians and Quebecers of African heritage toiled and endured injustice, never having
      been accorded the recognition they deserved - and yet paved the way for future generations to seize
      opportunity - first for freedom, next for education and ultimately, to achieve success;

      WHEREAS observance of Black History Month is an opportunity for our community and our schools to
      reflect on, commemorate and celebrate the contributions of African Canadians and Quebecers from all
      walks of life throughout our history;

      WHEREAS observance of Black History Month calls on the community to honour the contribution of
      those pioneers who by recognizing their own spirit, helped ensure our legacy of Civil Rights and Freedoms;

      WHEREAS we are inspired by the perseverance of our fellow citizens and wish to acknowledge this
      contribution to the noble goals and shared values of our country, as well, to re-examine and re-assert our
      contemporary role in preserving and defending the Civil Rights of all citizens;

      WHEREAS the English Montreal School Board recognizes Black History Month as an occasion for each
      of its schools to offer students an opportunity to understand the good will and common humanity
      underlying all people; to develop pride in his or her own identity and heritage; and to understand, respect,
      and accept the identity and heritage of others;

      WHEREAS corresponding school activities held during this month encourage students to continue to
      strive for the best education as a means to fulfill their own potential;

      WHEREAS not only during the month of February, but throughout the school year, it is important to
      recognize both disparities and opportunities in working towards greater equity in our schools, in the
      community and throughout the education system;

      IT IS MOVED BY MR. JULIEN FELDMAN AND UNANIMOUSLY RESOLVED THAT the English
      Montreal School Board designate February 2026 as Black History Month, observed in all of its schools
      and centres.

      Vote: 15-0-0, Adopted.
