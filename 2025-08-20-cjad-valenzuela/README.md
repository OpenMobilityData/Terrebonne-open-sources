## What the interview covers

The candidate's priorities for the borough (housing, security, basic services; the two halves of the borough); the Terrebonne bike path: the 2020 temporary lanes, the 2020–2021 working committee and its recommendations, « implemented … without consulting the residents », the audit her party proposed (« security … implementation … impact on local residents »); the McGill study on micromobility space allocation published the week before (« I don't think it's a comprehensive study »); services in NDG; the former borough mayor's candidacy. CJAD's description of the clip: « Stephanie Valenzuela, city councillor for the Darlington district in Côte-des-Neiges–Notre-Dame-de-Grâce will be running for borough mayor ».
