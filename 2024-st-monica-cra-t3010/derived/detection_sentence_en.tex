Given the year-to-year variation observed from 2015 to 2023, this record cannot be expected to detect a lane-related change in the parish's gifts smaller than about 15 to 26~\% of a year's gifts against the parish's own history, or about 14 to 16 points against the median change of the twelve neighbouring parishes (two standard deviations of the pre-lane changes, the lower figure excluding the three COVID transitions; section~\ref{sec:threshold}). Because the lane covered seven of the twelve months of 2024, a lane effect would have had to remove about 25 to 45~\% of the parish's full-year gifts, and about 21 to 50~\% of its total revenue, to stand apart in the 2024 return; the observed 2024 changes ($-$6.1~\% on gifts, $-$6.9 points against the peers) are well inside those thresholds. The 2025 return, a full year, would lower the threshold to about 14 to 26~\% of gifts, and two full years to about 10 to 18~\%.
