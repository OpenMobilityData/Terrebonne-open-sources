# Protocol: young cyclists killed on the island of Montréal, in the newspapers digitized by BAnQ

Version 1.0, November 16, 2026. Written and committed before the search. The author approved the four points marked « owner » on November 16, 2026 (« go »). Any later change goes in « Amendments » at the end, dated, with its reason.

## 1. Question

Have cyclists under 15 been killed in Montréal, how many does the press record, when and where? The recent public data cannot say (police and SAAQ open data carry no age; a press search of 2012–2020 found none confirmed inside the City: `Terrebonne-open-sources/2012-2026-montreal-area-young-road-users-press/`). The newspapers digitized by Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec cover more than a century.

The result is a **press record, not a census**: a death that no digitized paper reported, or that the character recognition garbled, will be missed. The report says so, and gives the counts by decade with that caution.

## 2. Definitions

- **Cyclist:** a person riding or pushing a bicycle or tricycle, or carried on one. Not a motorcyclist, not a pedestrian struck by a bicycle.
- **Killed:** died of the event, at the scene or later, as the paper reports it (a later article reporting the death counts).
- **Age (owner):** every cyclist under 18 found is recorded; under 15 is the main count. A report with no age is opened; if no article gives the age, the row is kept with age « not given ».
- **Place (owner):** the island of Montréal. The municipality of the time is recorded as printed (Verdun, Saint-Laurent, Westmount, Lachine and so on), so that the present City of Montréal can be separated afterwards. Events off the island are excluded and counted.
- **Period (owner):** everything the collection holds; results by decade.

## 3. Source and conduct

- BAnQ numérique (numerique.banq.qc.ca), « Revues et journaux », full-text search, restricted with the facet « Lieu de publication » to the papers published in Montréal (both spellings of the facet). The list of titles returned is recorded.
- Read in the author's Chrome at a reading pace: one results page at a time, no bulk download, no copy of page images. Optional cookies declined. If the site refuses or slows a request, stop and report; no route around it.
- French and English queries. Whether the engine honours quotation marks and OR is tested on the first query and recorded.

## 4. Queries (fixed here; each logged with the string as typed, the date, the count for all papers and for Montréal papers)

1. `"jeune cycliste" tué camion` (the pilot query)
2. `"jeune cycliste" tué automobile`
3. `"jeune cycliste" tué tramway`
4. `bicyclette garçonnet tué`
5. `bicyclette fillette tuée`
6. `bicyclette écolier tué`
7. `bicyclette enfant écrasé`
8. `bicyclette enfant happé`
9. `bécane garçonnet tué`
10. `cycliste "ans" "mort accidentelle" coroner`
11. `boy bicycle killed truck`
12. `"boy cyclist" killed`
13. `girl bicycle killed`

If a query returns more than 1,000 Montréal-paper pages, it is narrowed by adding one word (recorded as an amendment) rather than sampled.

## 5. Screening and reading

- **Snippets:** every result of every query is read as a snippet. Decision per result, logged: `candidate` (a cyclist's death, and the snippet does not show an age of 18 or more or a place off the island), or excluded with a code: `X1` off the island, `X2` aged 18 or over, `X3` not a cyclist, `X4` injured, not killed, `X5` not an event report, `X6` unreadable.
- **Pages:** the newspaper page of every candidate is opened and read. Recorded: date of the event, paper, date and page with its BAnQ address, age, sex as printed, place (street or intersection, municipality of the time), the other vehicle, the circumstances in a few neutral words, where the death occurred if stated.
- **Events:** reports of the same death in several papers or on several days are merged into one row, every page kept as a source.
- **Names** are in the newspapers. They are not recorded in the tables.

## 6. Outputs

`queries_log.csv`, `screening_log.csv` (every result with its decision), `events.csv` (one row per death, with its sources), a table by decade and by age group (under 10, 10 to 14, 15 to 17), and a README. Working copy in this folder; the public copy goes to a new folder of `Terrebonne-open-sources` (owner), with links to the BAnQ pages and no page images.

## 7. Checks

- The author checks a random sample of 20 events against the pages (seed 20261116, events sorted by date).
- The pilot's leads (section 8) must all be found again by the protocol's own screening; one that is not is reported.
