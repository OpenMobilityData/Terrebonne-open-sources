## The petitions themselves

The petitions are not published by the borough; they were released under the Access Act, with names, signatures and addresses blacked out, as access request [2025-081](https://github.com/OpenMobilityData/Terrebonne-access-requests/blob/main/2025-081/README.md) (companion repository). From that release:

- **Friday, February 2, 2024:** cover letters in French and English, marked « par courriel / main à main », addressed to the borough mayor and councillors and asking the clerk to place the petitions on the agenda of February 5 ([pp. 1–2](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2025-081/raw/Doc_requis_2025-081.pdf#page=1)). The French letter counts 306 online signatures and 1,102 in all; the English letter, 342 online and 1,138 in all, with « petitions signed by 796 citizens whom we met personally ».
- **Monday, February 5, 2024, 12:20:** a supplementary letter e-mailed to the clerk ([p. 67](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2025-081/raw/Doc_requis_2025-081.pdf#page=67)), adding 141 handwritten and 15 online signatures, « for a total of 937 residents » on paper and 321 online, « in total … 1,258 ».
- The petition text is headed « Pétition pour le vivre-ensemble dans le cadre de la configuration de rues, notamment celui de la rue de Terrebonne » ([p. 3](https://openmobilitydata.github.io/Terrebonne-access-requests/2025-081/raw/Doc_requis_2025-081.pdf#page=3)).
