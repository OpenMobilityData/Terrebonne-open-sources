# City council of Montréal, October 19, 2026: the questions on rue de Terrebonne

**Issued by:** Ville de Montréal, conseil municipal, ordinary sitting of October 19, 2026 (13:00), webcast on the City's YouTube channel. Speakers: Peter McQueen (city councillor, Notre-Dame-de-Grâce district) and Alexandre Teodoresco (city councillor, Loyola district) in the council members' question period; in the public question period, Hannah Bunty, David Gamper and James Bugden (residents of Côte-des-Neiges–Notre-Dame-de-Grâce, who put their questions in person), answered by Alan DeSousa (executive committee member responsible for mobility and infrastructure) and Alexandre Teodoresco; the council president in the chair.
**Date:** October 19, 2026.
**Source:** live-feed recording, https://www.youtube.com/live/rii3nUTuKRE (8 h 41 min). The times given are those of this recording; the City may replace it with an edited archival version whose times differ.
**Obtained:** audio of the full recording retrieved October 19, 2026 (evening) with yt-dlp; four sections of eight minutes cut from it. The recording is the City's and is not reproduced here: there is no `raw/` folder. The transcripts in `derived/` are published for the purposes of criticism and review (fair dealing, *Copyright Act*, s. 29).

## What the exchanges cover

The borough's announced winter conversion of one direction of the Terrebonne bike lane: the documents released under access request 2026-581 (the Service de l'urbanisme et de la mobilité's analysis of June 17, 2026 and the fire department's opinion), their publication, the City's review of the cycling network announced February 10, 2026 and whether its results would be published before the borough's vote, the winter counts, and the City's part in the reconfiguration.

## What was said (quotations, with the recording's time)

- Councillor Teodoresco, answering Peter McQueen (41:44): « On n'a aucun problème à partager nos documents parce qu'on s'appuie sur des données probantes aussi »; « une opération où on a enfoncé dans la gorge des résidents une perte de 250 places de stationnement »; « on passe à 3 % d'usage de cette piste cyclable pendant les mois de décembre, janvier, février et mars »; « on garde une ligne entière protégée pour la piste cyclable d'un côté et on remet des stationnements d'un autre côté […] uniquement pendant les mois ».
- Councillor Teodoresco, complementary answer (44:51): « on a annoncé très tôt dans le mandat 50 nouveaux compteurs de nouvelle génération »; « le seul compteur disponible, finalement, a été retiré du domaine public par votre administration »; « On parle de 30 passages par jour, 40 passages par jour. Dans les heures de pointe, on parle de 8 cyclistes, dont moi, j'en fais partie. »
- Alan DeSousa, answering Hannah Bunty (7:07:49): « je pense qu'ils vont partager cette information avec nous. Et puis, on vise d'amener ça au conseil municipal. »
- Councillor Teodoresco, answering Hannah Bunty's complementary question (7:10:02): « il n'y a pas une seule piste cyclable qui va être là, qui ne va pas être signée par les ingénieurs de la ville ou nos professionnels et qui ne va pas respecter les règlements et la loi »; « on est très content de cette décision ».
- Alan DeSousa, answering David Gamper (7:18:13): the four audits announced in February "will be made public as soon as we can compile the data"; "I initiated discussions with the different boroughs, of which Côte-des-Neiges-NDG was one of them, with review of particular bike paths that were issues in their community that had been brought up to us during the election"; the borough "will be sharing it with us […] after which we will be presenting a final file to Council" (7:19:13). On whether the review's findings would be published before the vote (7:19:53): "in all likelihood, will be as soon as possible, but it may not be before that"; the four reviews "will not necessarily mean that what is found […] will be immediately exportable or relevant or pertinent to the specificities that you might find on Terrebonne".
- Councillor Teodoresco, answering James Bugden (7:38:24): "we didn't see you yesterday at Café Citoyens where 40 of my residents were able to discuss, among other things, the Terrebonne bike path"; "we have years of daily counting data from counters across the borough and the city. It's not weak data. We've done a recent campaign. We went door to door, 4,000 doors"; "it's those only those four months during winter, when we're talking about 3.6 % of overall cycling traffic".

## How the transcripts were made

Each section was drafted with Whisper (whisper.cpp, large-v3-turbo) forced to French and to English, then every passage of about 20 seconds was listened to and corrected by Rick Hoge on a check page showing the audio beside the text (October 19–20, 2026). Passages before a question began or after an answer ended were left out. Speakers are named as they presented themselves or were called by the chair; the chair's interjections appear as their own turns. `[inaudible]` marks a word that could not be made out. The written question submitted by Rick Hoge for the same sitting is not included here.
