**David Gamper** (7:16:42)  
Bonsoir. Je suis ici en tant que membres de l'association des piétons et cyclistes NDG, comme la madame qui m'a précédé, qui compte environ 900 adhérents dans notre bel arrondissement. I will continue in English. We are very attached to our quite new cycle path on the rue de Terrebonne. It is the only east-west cycle route across NDG that is separated and is dual. On February the 10th this year, the city announced a review of its cycling network and named rue de Terrebonne among the paths it is reviewing. The time for results to be available was to be this fall, but nothing has emerged yet. However, on the 6th of October, a councillor of NDG announced that one direction would be taken away for the winter and announced that a vote would be taken on December the 7th. So my question to Mr. Alan DeSouza is, will the city ask CDN-NDG to wait for the results of the city's review and consultation before the Terrebonne configuration is changed?

**Alan DeSousa** (7:18:13)  
You're right, in January we did initiate audits for four particular parts, four particular bike paths, and those audits are ongoing and will be made public as soon as we can compile the data and make it available to the public. In the meantime, nothing precluded, and in fact I initiated discussions with the different boroughs, of which Côte-des-Neiges-NDG was one of them, with review of particular bike paths that were issues in their community that had been brought up to us during the election, that citizens that were there were concerned about, and believe me, there was no, for which there was no emerging consensus over the period of time that we witnessed it over the last eight years. So clearly the elected officials in Côte-des-Neiges-NDG have done, over the past year, their review, their analysis of what is in their community, they've interfaced with the different people and the groups, whether it be the schools, the churches, et cetera, as to what might be most appropriate, and they've come up with a solution that can provide for the best possible equilibrium To meet the needs of all the users in the community, and that's something that they are bringing forward to their Council. At the same time as they brought it to their Council, they'll be sharing it with us, and clearly, we will hope that all it's done with rigor, and at the same time, if there's any additional suggestions, we work together hand in hand, and after which we will be presenting a final file to Council.

And so, whereas those informations will be made public, but in the meantime, the effort that is done by the by… by the Côte-des-Neiges-NDG Borough will be done with the amount of rigor, taking into account the safety needs of their community and the mobility needs of all the users in your community. And that's something that will be relevant.
