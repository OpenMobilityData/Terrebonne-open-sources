## What the documents say

**Why two councils.** Rue de Terrebonne is on the City's arterial road network (RAAV) and the lane is on the cycling network of the Plan vélo 2019, both under the jurisdiction of the City council (the latter by delegation from the agglomeration council, CG23 0749). Under the second paragraph of article 85 of the Charter of Ville de Montréal, a borough council may offer to provide a service within the City council's jurisdiction; "la résolution du conseil d'arrondissement prend effet à compter de l'adoption par le conseil de la ville d'une résolution acceptant la fourniture de services" (City file, p. 2).

**Borough council, February 5, 2024.** Item 30.02, resolution **CA24 170018**, "Offre au conseil municipal – aménagement cyclable – rue de Terrebonne": "D'offrir, au conseil municipal, la prise en charge de la conception et de la réalisation des travaux pour un aménagement cyclable rehaussé sur la rue de Terrebonne, en vertu de l'article 85 alinéa 2 de la Charte de la Ville de Montréal." Moved by Gracia Kasoki Katahwa, seconded by Despina Sourias. "Un débat s'engage." Magda Popeanu, Despina Sourias and Peter McQueen voted for; Stephanie Valenzuela and Sonny Moroz voted against. "Adoptée à la majorité" (minutes, pp. 13–14). The minutes also list the public questions on the project at that sitting.

The borough's sommaire décisionnel (file 1246880002, signed by the borough director on January 31, 2024) describes the project: 2.5 km between Belmore and Girouard; the existing facility "se limite à une chaussée désignée et la circulation véhiculaire est permise dans les 2 directions"; the proposal is unidirectional protected lanes along the whole length, "d'un côté par des bollards et de l'autre par la bande de stationnement restante", with the street made one-way, westbound from Belmore to Cavendish and eastbound from Cavendish to Girouard, "élément essentiel au projet"; parking prohibited within 5 m of intersections; signal adjustments at Cavendish, Grand, Draper and Girouard; "L'aménagement cyclable sera de type ''transitoire'' et n'impliquera aucun travail de voirie. Les travaux sont composés principalement de changements de marquage et de signalisation"; deployment planned for May–June 2024; estimated cost $250,000, borne by the SUM's operating budget; the project is SUM_CDN24-0907 under the SUM's 2023–2024 call for projects (result communicated January 16, 2024); the project was announced by press release on October 30, 2023 and an information session was held on November 30, 2023; the calendar foresees "Février 2024 : Le CM devra accepter l'offre du CA". The attachments are EXP's preliminary design plans of August 18, 2023 (MTR-00251766-A5), reproduced in reduced form in the bundle.

**City council, February 19, 2024.** Item 30.06, resolution **CM24 0179**, on the executive committee's recommendation CE24 0210 of February 14, 2024: "d'accepter les offres de services des conseils d'arrondissement de prendre en charge la coordination et la réalisation des travaux d'aménagement de rues artérielles et de voies cyclables pour l'année 2024 conformément au 2e alinéa de l'article 85 de la Charte". Moved by François Limoges, seconded by Luc Rabouin, "Adopté à l'unanimité" (minutes, pp. 67–68). The resolution covers all boroughs' 2024 offers at once and does not name Terrebonne; the City file lists the projects, among them "SUM_CDN24-0907 – Aménagement cyclable rehaussé sur la rue Terrebonne" with $220,000 in the column "BF Vélo" (the SUM's operating budget for cycling), alongside the borough's other 2024 offers (Promenade Jean-Brillant, Cavendish fences, avenue Bourret one-way, avenue Légaré). The City council minutes also record public questions on the project at the February 19 sitting and at the following one.

For the petitions tabled with the borough three days before the February 5 sitting, see the companion repository, access request 2025-081.
